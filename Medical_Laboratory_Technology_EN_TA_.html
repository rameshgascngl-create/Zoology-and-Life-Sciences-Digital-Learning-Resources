Academic reference framework

  • National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP) approved DMLT curriculum, 2026 — theory, practicals and clinical-application courses.
  • WHO Laboratory Biosafety Manual, Fourth Edition (2020) and implementation guidance — risk-assessment-based biosafety.
  • CDC Standard Precautions and core infection-prevention practices.
  • WHO malaria microscopy, blood safety and quality-assurance guidance.
  • Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016, as amended from time to time, together with applicable institutional/state procedures.
  • Current institutional SOPs, manufacturer instructions and authorised professional supervision take precedence for operational parameters.
