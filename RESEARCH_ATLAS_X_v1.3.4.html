Word Import & Export Centre

Import Microsoft Word (.docx) content into Research Inbox for organisation, and generate genuine .docx working documents from the active research project. Both operations remain local in your browser.

Import Word Content

Local .docx extraction

Choose a Microsoft Word .docx file. RESEARCH ATLAS X extracts the main document text and table-cell text locally, then sends it to Research Inbox for review, classification and organisation.

The original Word file remains on your device. The app stores the extracted text and source-file metadata in the active project. Password-protected, encrypted, legacy .doc and malformed DOCX files cannot be extracted.

Choose Research Output

The export organises data already entered in RESEARCH ATLAS X. It does not invent thesis prose, verify scientific claims, or replace scholarly review. Citations and bibliography entries remain the scholar's responsibility to verify.
