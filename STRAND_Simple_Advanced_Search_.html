Your search
Important: No single database contains every published paper. Search more than one source before concluding that research is absent. Not finding a paper in these searches does not mean that no research exists.
Different research websites interpret keywords differently, so the same search may produce different results on each site.
The same paper may appear in more than one database. Count the paper once, not once per website.
For life-science topics, start with Europe PMC and PubMed. Then widen your search using Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex. Use Simple Search first; switch to Advanced Search only when you get too many results.
The region keyword is included in all main database searches. The separate India keyword search uses the base topic with “India” instead. Year limits are added automatically to Europe PMC, PubMed and Google Scholar. On other sites, apply the site's own year filters where available.
A region or country keyword only searches for that word in searchable records. It does not verify where the research was actually conducted.
Each site controls its own search page, so exact link behaviour (especially year filters) may change over time. An internet connection is needed when you open a result, and some publisher sites may need a subscription or institutional login for full text.
