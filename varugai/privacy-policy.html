Privacy Policy — VARUGAI Attendance Ledger

App: VARUGAI Attendance Ledger
Android package: com.gasczoology.varugai
Effective date: 23 September 2026

VARUGAI is an offline attendance ledger for teachers. It is an Android application, not a live website opened in a browser.

1. Information stored on the device

When you type or import it, VARUGAI stores class details, student names, roll or register numbers, working-day calendars, hour-wise attendance, eligibility settings and a correction log. That data stays in the app on this device.

2. Offline operation

Core marking works with no internet. The app does not require the INTERNET permission for its attendance functions.

3. What we do not do

VARUGAI does not create an online account, does not show third-party ads, and does not send the register to a developer-operated server. There is no analytics SDK in the current store build.

4. Files you export

Excel, CSV, JSON backups and printed statements are created only when you tap Export, Save or Print. Android’s document picker or print sheet lets you choose the destination. Protect exported copies the same way you protect a paper register.

5. Permissions

This version does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location. Optional app-lock uses the PIN you set and, if available, the device biometric prompt. Those stay on the phone.

6. Retention and deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove the on-device register. Exported files you already saved are not deleted by uninstalling. Teachers and institutions remain responsible for lawful use of student information.
