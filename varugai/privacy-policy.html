Privacy Policy — VARUGAI Attendance Ledger

App: VARUGAI Attendance Ledger
Android package: com.gasczoology.varugai
Effective date: 22 September 2026

VARUGAI Attendance Ledger is an offline-first application intended for teachers, departments and academic institutions to record, manage and review student attendance.

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on how the user configures the app, VARUGAI may store information such as student names, roll numbers or identifiers, class or course details, attendance records, period-wise attendance, practical-session attendance, notes, and user-selected or captured photo evidence associated with attendance records.

2. Local storage and offline operation

VARUGAI is designed to operate offline. Attendance and roster data are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not require continuous internet access to perform its core attendance functions.

3. Data transmission

VARUGAI does not transmit attendance records, student rosters, or stored photo evidence to developer-operated servers. The app does not sell personal information.

4. Backup and export

VARUGAI allows the user to create or export backups and reports. Such files are created only when the user initiates the relevant action. After export, the user is responsible for choosing where the file is stored, copied, uploaded or shared.

5. Camera, files and photo evidence

If the user chooses to add photo-based evidence or import a file, VARUGAI may invoke Android's system camera, photo picker or document picker as appropriate. Files or images selected by the user are used for the requested attendance or import function and are not uploaded to developer-operated servers by VARUGAI.

6. Accounts, advertising and analytics

VARUGAI does not require users to create an online account for its core functions. The app is not designed to display third-party advertising or to use third-party behavioural advertising trackers. It is also not designed to send classroom attendance data to third-party analytics services.

7. Student information and institutional responsibility

VARUGAI is a record-keeping tool for authorised academic use. Teachers and institutions using the app are responsible for entering student information lawfully, protecting exported files, controlling access to the device, and following the privacy, consent, retention and attendance-record rules applicable to their institution.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored in VARUGAI remains on the user's device until it is deleted through the app, removed by clearing application data, or removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to any backups or exported copies independently retained by the user.

9. Security

VARUGAI is designed to minimise unnecessary data exposure by keeping its core attendance data on the user's device. Users should protect their device with an appropriate screen lock and store exported backups in a secure location.

10. Children's and students' data

VARUGAI is intended for use by teachers and authorised academic personnel rather than for direct use by children. Where student information is entered into the app, the responsible teacher or institution should ensure that its use complies with applicable institutional policies and legal requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data-handling practices change. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are made.
