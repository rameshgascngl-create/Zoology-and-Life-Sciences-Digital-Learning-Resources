Privacy Policy – Ornamental Fish Science

Effective date: 22 September 2026

Summary: Ornamental Fish Science is an offline-first educational application for learning ornamental fish biology, aquarium care, breeding and related practical topics. The application does not require a user account and is not designed to collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.

Application identity

App: Ornamental Fish Science
Android package: com.tnfisheries.ornamental
Category: Education

Information collection

The application does not require users to provide a name, email address, telephone number, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photographs or other personal information.

Offline use and device permissions

The educational content is designed to work locally on the device. The application is not designed to access the camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, phone/SMS functions or other sensitive device data for its core educational functions.

Local preferences and learning progress

The application may store limited information locally on the user's device, such as selected language, bookmarks, quiz progress, reading state or other learning preferences. This local information is used only to preserve the user's learning experience and is not designed to be transmitted to the developer.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The application does not contain third-party advertising and is not designed to use behavioural advertising, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Data sharing and sale

Because the application is not designed to collect personal information, it does not sell personal data or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Privacy Policy webpage

The application's Privacy Policy link may open this public webpage in the user's web browser. This webpage is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a browser accesses a GitHub-hosted webpage, GitHub may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser information and server logs according to GitHub's own privacy practices. This browser-hosting activity is separate from the application's offline educational functions.

Children and students

The application is intended for educational use by students, teachers and learners interested in ornamental fish science. It does not require account creation or the submission of personal information. Users under the applicable age of digital consent should use online services under the supervision required by their parent, guardian or institution.

Security and policy changes

The application is designed to keep its educational content and limited preferences on the device. If a future version introduces features that change how information is handled, this Privacy Policy and the applicable app-store data-safety disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Contact
