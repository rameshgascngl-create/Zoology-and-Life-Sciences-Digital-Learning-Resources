Privacy Policy – Environmental Studies

Effective date: 1 September 2026

Summary: Environmental Studies is designed as an offline educational application. It does not require a user account and is not designed to collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information collection

The application does not require users to provide a name, email address, telephone number, account credentials, location, contacts, photographs, or other personal information.

Device permissions

The application is designed to operate without access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone/SMS, or user file storage. It does not require an Internet permission for its core educational content.

Local preferences

The application may save limited preferences locally on the user's device, such as the selected language and reading position/page. These preferences are used only to provide continuity in the learning experience and are not transmitted to the developer.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The application does not contain advertising and is not designed to use analytics, behavioural tracking, or third-party advertising trackers.

Data sharing and sale

The application is not designed to collect personal data; consequently, it does not sell or share personal data with advertisers or other third parties.

Children and students

The application provides educational material for undergraduate Environmental Studies. It does not require account creation or submission of personal information.

Security and changes

The application is designed to keep its educational content and limited preferences on the device. This policy may be updated if future versions introduce features that change data handling. Any such changes should be reflected in the application's Google Play Data safety declarations and this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions about the application, users may contact the developer through the developer contact information displayed on the application's Google Play Store listing.
